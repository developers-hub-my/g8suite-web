g8deck takes an architecture blueprint and deploys it onto infrastructure you
own — then keeps it running: drift is detected and reconciled, releases roll
back, environments promote through gates, and databases, caches and object
storage are managed on your own servers with backups you can restore. Take it as
a managed console or run it yourself.
