G8ID governs who holds which access across the organisation — every joiner,
mover and leaver passes through an approval gate, directories are checked for
drift, and access is recertified on a schedule. It is identity governance and
administration, in the space occupied by SailPoint and Saviynt, and runs
entirely inside your perimeter.
